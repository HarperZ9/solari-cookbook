# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Engineers investigating broken workflows, and reviewers evaluating how an agent arrived at a repair. The first user has one small monitor and needs concise navigation. A run should remain understandable after leaving and returning.

## Product Purpose

Help a person investigate a failure, compare competing repairs, challenge the apparent winner and inspect the resulting application. This is a Flywheel application using Solari execution environments. The outcome is a runnable candidate with understandable evidence and explicit unresolved questions.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, human. Use the established Zentropy/Telos visual identity and plain technical language. Explain what happened and what the reader can do next.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic marketing dashboards, giant counters, repeated decorative cards, noisy animated backgrounds, tiny labels and exaggerated claims. Can.ac is an evidence-density reference for publications, not a product-layout template to copy.

## Design Principles

- Keep the active experiment, its result and the relevant source change close together.
- Let the reader expand detail instead of making every trace visible at once.
- Distinguish observed behavior, model suggestions and untested work.
- Preserve readable results when scripts or network services are unavailable.
- Keep the full mission navigable without consuming the user's whole screen.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic headings and controls, visible keyboard focus, AA contrast, 320px reflow, text-spacing tolerance, reduced motion, forced colors and print support. Color always has a text equivalent. No flashing, autoplay or interaction-dependent access to essential evidence.

## Context authority

Derived from the user-approved R&D direction, project-docs/specs/SPEC-rd-lab.md, the user's small-monitor and readability requirements, and the existing Zentropy design canon. Cloud execution and model-driven repairs remain unfinished; do not use the interface to imply they have run.
